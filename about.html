Who we are

A local nonprofit working for a more inclusive community.

The Sharon Housing Trust is a private nonprofit corporation operated by local volunteers. It is focused on increasing the availability of affordable housing in Sharon, a rural, historic town in an expensive corner of Connecticut.

The Trust’s work is rooted in a simple belief: people who contribute to the life of Sharon should have a chance to make a home here.

Our mission

Increase affordable housing in Sharon.

The Trust creates and preserves homes that can remain affordable over time, strengthening the community and helping more people call Sharon home.

How we work

Housing grows through collaboration.

The Trust works with town government, regional nonprofits, the state, foundations, and individuals. Local volunteers bring these partners together around housing needs and practical opportunities to act.

A helpful distinction

The Trust is not the Sharon Housing Authority.

The Sharon Housing Trust is a private nonprofit corporation run by local volunteers. It is a separate organization from the Sharon Housing Authority.
