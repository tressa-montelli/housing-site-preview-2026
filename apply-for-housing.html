Rental application guidance

Start with the Trust’s application information.

The Trust says rental applicants will be best positioned if their household income is 80% of Area Median Income or lower. This is guidance only and does not guarantee eligibility or a housing opportunity.

The current application page also links to an interest form for affordable housing in Sharon and surrounding towns.
