Eligibility

Who can apply?

  • First-time homebuyers: no household member may have held an ownership interest in a principal residence at any time during the past three years.
  • The home must be occupied as the buyer’s primary residence.
  • Total household gross annual income must be at or below 100% of the Litchfield County AMI. Household assets will also be assessed.
  • Applicants must pre-qualify for a mortgage of at least $200,000.
  • Because the home has three bedrooms, households of three or more receive a strong preference.

Getting started

How to pre-apply

  1. Complete and sign the pre-application form.
  2. Include a mortgage pre-qualification letter showing qualification for a mortgage of at least $200,000. The packet says this letter is required for the pre-application to be accepted.
  3. Submit both documents by email to LindsayL@thehousingcollective.org or mail them to Sharon Housing Trust, Inc., P.O. Box 1168, Sharon, CT 06069.

If your pre-application is approved, you will be asked to submit a full application package with income and asset documents, such as tax returns, paycheck stubs, and bank statements. If multiple eligible households apply, the buyer will be selected in a lottery drawing.
