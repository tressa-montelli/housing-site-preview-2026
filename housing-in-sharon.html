Why housing affordability matters

When housing takes too much, the whole household feels it.

A household is considered housing cost burdened when it spends more than 30% of its income on housing. That can leave less for food, health care, transportation, and the other costs of daily life.

380Sharon households are housing cost burdened.HUD CHAS, as cited in the Trust’s October 2026 presentation

Who is affected?

Affordable housing serves our neighbors.

About 400 Sharon households earn below 80% of Area Median Income. That includes seniors, young professionals, families, fire and EMS volunteers, and many others.

Affordable housing helps make room for people at different stages of life and with different kinds of work.
